
## 2026-10-17 competitor-informed UI pass

A live production review and comparison of Carlcare, iFixit, uBreakiFix/Asurion, Apple Service and Repair, Samsung Repair Services and Jumia Kenya found that the strongest patterns are task-first entry points, device/problem selection, visible trust promises, simple repair tracking, and commerce cards that do not compete with repair intake. Highbridge’s previous first viewport was too dark and decorative: the real service-desk image was obscured, the navigation was visually busy, and the hierarchy felt like generated marketing copy.

The latest UI pass changed the public homepage to a practical service-desk layout: a warm light canvas, a visible real service-desk photograph, shorter and more specific first-viewport wording, separate repair and shop actions, three trust checkpoints, symptom-first repair cards, distinct repair-versus-parts routes, and a dark FAQ band for contrast. The shared public navigation was simplified into a light, readable header with a restrained orange brand accent and clearer mobile actions. This pass was typechecked, tested, built, and visually inspected locally before publication.

When continuing visual work, compare the rendered route at desktop and mobile sizes first. Do not return to neon-on-black hero compositions, oversized abstract headings, or image backgrounds that hide the subject. Improve task clarity, real imagery, product discovery, trust and conversion before adding more copy.
