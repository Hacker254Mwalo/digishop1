# Customer trust and review-request playbook

## Non-negotiable policy

Ask for an honest review from every customer after a genuine repair or purchase experience—not only from customers who seem pleased. Never offer cash, airtime, discounts, free accessories, a warranty extension, or any other benefit in exchange for a review, a particular star rating, editing a review, or removing criticism. A thank-you for taking time to share feedback is fine; it must not depend on what the customer says or rates.

Do not publish staff-written, AI-written, or invented testimonials as customer reviews. Do not pressure people to review on the spot, filter complaints away from public sites, or ask for a positive review. Do not add a Google-review link until the owner has copied and verified the link from the actual Digital Phone Hub Business Profile. Replace `[VERIFIED REVIEW LINK]` only after that check.

Google's policy prohibits incentives and selective solicitation. Google's local-ranking guidance says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence; review volume is not a guaranteed number-one ranking lever.

## Neutral request templates

### WhatsApp after a completed repair

> Hi [first name], this is Digital Phone Hub in Siaya. We have completed the [device/model, if appropriate] repair discussed with you. If you have a moment, would you share an honest review of your experience? Your feedback helps people in Siaya make an informed choice. [VERIFIED REVIEW LINK] No rating or wording is expected. If anything still needs attention, please reply here and we will look into it.

### WhatsApp after a parts purchase

> Hi [first name], thanks for choosing Digital Phone Hub. We hope the [item] is the right fit for your device. If you would like, you can share an honest review of your experience here: [VERIFIED REVIEW LINK]. Your review is entirely optional and there is no reward tied to it. If you have a compatibility or product question, reply to this message and we will help.

### Short printed receipt / counter card

> Thank you for choosing Digital Phone Hub, Siaya. Your honest feedback—positive, mixed or critical—helps the next customer decide. Leave a review: [VERIFIED REVIEW LINK]. A review is optional and is not linked to any discount, gift or service benefit. Need follow-up help? Call 0718 114 009 and quote your reference.

### Service follow-up when the customer is not ready to review

> No problem—there is no need to leave a review. If you need help with the device or the repair result, reply here or call 0718 114 009 and we will check the next step with you.

### Response to a positive public review

> Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. We appreciate you choosing Digital Phone Hub in Siaya. If you need help again, contact the service desk on 0718 114 009.

### Response to a negative or mixed public review

> Thank you for raising this. We are sorry the experience did not meet expectations. We would like to understand what happened and check the right next step. Please contact the service desk on 0718 114 009 and quote your reference. For your privacy, please do not post your phone number or repair details publicly.

Never include a customer's name, phone, device fault, reference, payment details, or repair history in a public reply unless the customer has explicitly made those details public and has asked for them to be discussed.

## Trust-building incentives that do not buy reviews

Use benefits that are available independently of whether anyone leaves a review. Examples, only if the owner actually offers and can honour them, include a clearly described referral benefit, a regular-customer offer, a free device-care checklist, or a short post-repair check-in. Publish the eligibility, duration and exclusions; do not condition the benefit on posting, editing, or deleting a review. Do not advertise a warranty extension, free check, airtime, or gift until the owner has approved the exact terms and can fulfil them.

The strongest trust features already supported by the site's actual workflow are a repair reference, status-only tracking, a clear request history for the owner, compatibility confirmation before a parts request is treated as a purchase, and owner-confirmed repair quotations before repair work begins. Keep those service promises accurate and consistently honoured.

## Suggested operating routine

1. After the repair is marked complete or an item is handed over, send the same neutral request to all eligible customers.
2. Keep review requests separate from complaint handling. Resolve the support issue whether or not the customer posts a review.
3. Ask once; do not repeatedly message customers who ignore the request.
4. Keep a record of support follow-up, not of customers' star ratings or whether they were selected because of sentiment.
5. Review the current Business Profile policy and the verified profile link before changing the templates.
